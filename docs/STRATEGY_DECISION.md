# Strategy Decision

## Selected direction
**Pilot -> Evidence -> Productization**

## Primary wedge
Technical architecture and design-review meetings for software and fintech teams.

## Why this wins now
- Directly matches the strongest part of the existing product: question clustering, prioritization, and human moderation.
- Easy to demonstrate in a controlled meeting.
- Questions have high economic value in architecture/design decisions.
- Produces high-value moderator decisions that can become evaluation data.
- Avoids spending the next milestone on speculative infrastructure.

## Secondary tracks
AI moat and production hardening remain supporting tracks, triggered by pilot evidence.

## Stage gate
Do not scale infrastructure materially until at least three real meetings have produced evidence that moderators trust the nominees and that manual triage time decreases.
