# Netherlands Startup Evidence Roadmap

This document is a product evidence plan, not legal advice and not a prediction of an immigration outcome.

## Official route dimensions to support

The current IND startup page (last updated 8 June 2026) states that the startup route includes an innovative business, an active founder role, a reliable facilitator, and a step-by-step plan; it also describes KVK registration and financial-resource requirements. RVO advises on the facilitator, innovativeness, and the step-by-step plan.

## MeetLens evidence mapping

### Innovation
Evidence to build:
- collective question-intelligence workflow
- semantic compression with diversity protection
- moderator-controlled decision gate
- evidence-backed perspective/coverage signals
- privacy-first/local inference architecture

### Active founder role
Document:
- architecture ownership
- AI/pipeline implementation
- evaluation methodology
- product development decisions
- pilot leadership

### Step-by-step plan
Phase 0: working MVP
Phase 1: Zero Pilot
Phase 2: first external demonstrations
Phase 3: three-meeting controlled pilot
Phase 4: repeatable customer discovery and productization
Phase 5: Dutch market and facilitator process
