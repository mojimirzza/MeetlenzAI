# MeetLens — Netherlands Startup Evidence Map (2026)

This is product/evidence planning, not legal advice or a prediction of immigration approval.

## Current official route signals

As of 8 June 2026, the Dutch IND startup page states that a startup residence application involves an innovative business, an active founder role, a reliable facilitator under a signed agreement, a step-by-step plan, Trade Register registration, and sufficient means of support. The IND asks the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (RVO) for advice on innovativeness, the step-by-step plan, facilitator and registration matters.

RVO states that the facilitator must have experience mentoring innovative startups, be financially healthy, not hold a majority interest in the startup, and provide tailored support under a signed agreement.
