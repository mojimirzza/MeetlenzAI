# MeetLens Product Mission

## Primary wedge
Technical architecture and design-review meetings for software/fintech teams, where limited meeting time and high question density make information prioritization valuable.

## Core user
A participant who wants their question represented accurately without being drowned out by duplicates or louder voices.

## Buyer
Engineering/product leadership running recurring expert meetings.

## Moderator
The person responsible for the meeting agenda and final question selection.

## Pain
Important technical questions get duplicated, buried, or ignored. Moderators spend time manually grouping questions instead of resolving the highest-value information gaps.

## Product mechanism
MeetLens converts distributed questions into intent clusters, diverse nominees, transparent priority, and a moderator-controlled decision queue.

## Magic moment
`50 participant questions -> 8 intents -> 16 nominees -> moderator approves the highest-value questions.`

## Pilot hypothesis
MeetLens can reduce semantic redundancy while maintaining participant coverage and moderator control, and can reduce time spent manually triaging questions.

## Initial success metrics
- duplicate suppression rate
- moderator acceptance rate
- moderator edit rate
- participant coverage across selected intents
- time from intake to moderation
- answered/high-usefulness rate

## Product boundary
No transcript summarization as the primary value proposition. No autonomous asking. No autonomous moderation.
