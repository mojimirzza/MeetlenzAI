# Evidence-backed Blind Spot Radar

## Why it exists

MeetLens should not only find the most popular information need. In a meeting, an important perspective can be underrepresented even when the overall question volume is high.

## What the feature does

The radar compares:

- declared participant expertise
- submitted question expertise tags
- participating users
- resulting category coverage

It highlights expertise areas with low observed question coverage for moderator review.

## Safety boundary

The radar is an evidence-backed signal, not an assertion that the missing perspective is objectively necessary. It does not invent a concern, create an autonomous question, or alter priority without moderator action.

## Why it is strategically relevant

This feature directly extends the product thesis from "compress questions" to "preserve and expose collective information coverage". It is therefore a supporting differentiator, not a generic assistant feature.
